What is Hajj?

Hajj is the annual pilgrimage to Makkah and one of the five pillars of Islam. It is performed during Dhul Hijjah and includes sacred rituals such as Ihram, standing at Arafat, Muzdalifah stay, Mina rituals, Rami Al-Jamarat, Qurbani, and Tawaf Al-Ifada.

It represents devotion, unity, sacrifice, and submission to Allah, bringing millions of Muslims from around the world together each year.
