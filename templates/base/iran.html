OVERVIEW

What is Iran Ziyarat?

Iran Ziyarat includes visiting sacred shrines of Ahlul Bayt (AS), especially the shrine of Imam رضا (AS) in Mashhad and Hazrat Masuma (SA) in Qom.

It is a journey of spiritual reflection, seeking blessings, and strengthening connection with the family of the Prophet (PBUH).
