What is Sa'i?

Sa'i is walking between the hills of Safa and Marwah seven times. It commemorates the struggle of Hajar (AS) as she searched for water for baby Ismail (AS).

إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ

Translation: "Indeed, Safa and Marwah are among the symbols of Allah." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:158)

Purpose of Sa'i

Sa'i represents patience, trust in Allah, and effort. It is a pillar of Umrah and must be completed after Tawaf.

Rules of Sa'i

These rules ensure your Sa'i is valid.

Fard (Mandatory) Rules

1. Sa'i after Tawaf

Sa'i is only valid after completing Tawaf.

2. Start at Safa and end at Marwah

Seven complete laps: Safa → Marwah is 1 lap, Marwah → Safa is 2, and so on.

3. Walking the correct path

You must stay in the Sa'i corridor.

Sunnah Actions

Dhikr and Dua

Recite duas and dhikr throughout.

Ramal (Men)

Men jog between the green lights.

How to Perform Sa'i

Before Starting Sa'i

Step 1: Complete Tawaf

Sa'i begins only after Tawaf.

Step 2: Go to Safa

Face the Kaaba and make dua.

The 7 Rounds

Step 3: Make intention

“O Allah, I am performing Sa'i for Your sake.”

Step 4: Start walking Safa → Marwah

This counts as your first lap.

Step 5: Men jog between the green lights

This is Sunnah for men only.

Step 6: Reach Marwah

Make dua, then return toward Safa.

Step 7: Continue until you complete 7 laps

Finish at Marwah.

Duas During Sa'i

Dua at Safa

الله أكبر، الله أكبر، الله أكبر، لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له، له الملك وله الحمد وهو على كل شيء قدير

Dua at Marwah

رب اغفر وارحم إنك أنت الأعز الأكرم

General Dua

You may recite Quran, dhikr, or any dua from your heart.
