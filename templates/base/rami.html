HAJJ RITUAL

Rami

The ritual of stoning the Jamarat as part of the sacred journey of Hajj.

What is Rami?

Rami refers to the ritual of throwing pebbles at the Jamarat pillars. It is an important ritual performed during the days of Hajj.

The Jamarat

The Jamarat area contains the designated pillars where pilgrims perform the stoning ritual according to the prescribed Hajj schedule.

  • Use the required number of pebbles according to the ritual.
  • Follow the official Hajj schedule and instructions.
  • Remain patient and respectful around other pilgrims.
  • Keep safety and crowd-management instructions in mind.

Meaning of Rami

The ritual reminds pilgrims of obedience, faith and rejection of evil while completing their sacred journey.
