Masjid al-Nimrah

Masjid al-Nimrah is located on the plain of Arafat. It was at this site that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ stayed and delivered his historic Farewell Sermon before leading the prayers on the day of Hajj.

On the 9th of Dhul Hijjah, millions of pilgrims gather here to listen to the Hajj sermon. It is a Sunnah to combine the Dhuhr and Asr prayers at this mosque during the afternoon of Arafat.

Key Features & Etiquette

  • The Sermon: The Grand Mufti delivers the global Hajj message from here.
  • Capacity: One of the largest mosques, holding over 350,000 pilgrims.
  • Boundary: Part of the mosque is inside Arafat and part is outside.

Prophetic Legacy

This is where the Prophet ﷺ camped before the Wuquf.

Unity

The sermon is translated into multiple languages globally.

Important

Staying in the Wadi Urana section does not count as Wuquf-e-Arafat.
