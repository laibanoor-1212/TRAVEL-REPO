Imam Raza Shrine (Mashhad)

The shrine of Imam Raza (A.S) in Mashhad is one of the most sacred pilgrimage sites in Iran. It is the resting place of Imam Ali ibn Musa al-Rida (A.S), the 8th Imam of Islam.

Millions of pilgrims visit every year to seek blessings, spiritual peace, and connection with the Ahlulbayt (A.S).

Spiritual Significance

  • Burial place of Imam Raza (A.S).
  • One of the largest shrine complexes in the world.
  • A center of Islamic learning and spirituality.
  • Visited by millions annually.
