Masjid al-Khandaq

Masjid al-Khandaq is situated at the foot of Mount Sela in Madinah. It is a modern, large mosque built on the site where the Battle of the Trench (Ghazwat al-Khandaq) took place in the fifth year of Hijra.

The site is historically significant for the strategy suggested by Salman al-Farsi (RA), where a deep trench was dug to defend the city against an invading confederacy. This area originally housed several small mosques representing the command posts of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his companions.

Today, the large central Masjid al-Khandaq accommodates thousands of worshippers. The surrounding area still preserves the essence of the historical battlefield, serving as a powerful reminder of the unity, strategy, and divine help granted to the early Muslims.

Visiting Guidelines

  • Visit the site during the day to appreciate the geographical layout of the battlefield.
  • Perform your obligatory or voluntary prayers in the spacious main hall.
  • Observe the remaining smaller historical mosques nearby from a distance.
  • Reflect on the strategic patience and hardships faced during the siege of Madinah.
