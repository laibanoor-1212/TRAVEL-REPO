Cave Hira (Ghar-e-Hira)

Cave Hira is a small cave located near the top of Jabal al-Nour (The Mountain of Light). It is situated about 3 km from Makkah and is the site where Prophet Muhammad ﷺ spent long periods in meditation and prayer.

This location holds supreme importance in Islamic history because it was here in 610 CE that the Angel Jibrail (AS) brought the first revelation. The opening verses of Surah Al-Alaq ("Iqra") were revealed at this very spot.

The interior of the cave is small, fitting only a few people at a time. Interestingly, the cave faces the Holy Kaaba, which can be seen from the mountain peak.

Climbing & Visiting Tips

  • The climb takes approximately 45 to 90 minutes; wear comfortable walking shoes.
  • Carry a water bottle to stay hydrated during the steep trek.
  • It is best to climb during the early morning or evening to avoid the intense heat.
  • Be cautious on the rocky path and stay focused on the trail.
