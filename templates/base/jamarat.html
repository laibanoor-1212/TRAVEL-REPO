Jamarat (Stoning of the Devil)

Jamarat is one of the most important rituals of Hajj performed in Mina, near Makkah. Pilgrims symbolically throw pebbles at three pillars representing Shaytan (the devil), following the Sunnah of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) who rejected Satan’s temptations.

This ritual symbolizes the rejection of evil, purification of the soul, and complete submission to Allah. It takes place over three days of Hajj (11th, 12th, and 13th Dhul Hijjah).

Religious Significance

  • Following Ibrahim (AS): Re-enactment of Prophet Ibrahim’s rejection of Shaytan.
  • Spiritual Cleansing: Represents removal of sins and evil thoughts.
  • Obedience to Allah: Demonstrates complete submission to divine command.
  • Hajj Ritual: Essential part of the completion of Hajj pilgrimage.

SPIRITUAL LESSONS

Reject Evil

Symbolically rejecting Shaytan and all forms of temptation in life.

FAITH

Obedience

Showing complete obedience to Allah’s commands without hesitation.

Inner Strength

Building spiritual strength to resist sins and worldly distractions.
