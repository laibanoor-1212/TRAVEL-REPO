Jannat al-Mu'alla

Jannat al-Mu'alla is the oldest and most historic cemetery in Makkah, located near the Masjid al-Haram. Also known as "Al-Hajun," this graveyard existed even before the advent of Islam and holds immense religious significance today.

This sacred ground is the resting place of many prominent members of Prophet Muhammad's ﷺ family and several Sahaba (companions). Hazrat Khadija (RA), the first wife of the Prophet ﷺ and the "Mother of the Believers," is buried here.

Millions of pilgrims visiting for Hajj and Umrah come here to pay their respects and offer prayers for the noble souls who played a pivotal role in the early days of Islam.

Visiting Guidelines

  • Recite the prescribed supplication (Dua) when entering the cemetery.
  • Maintain silence and a high level of respect at all times.
  • Avoid placing flowers or lighting candles on the graves.
  • Reflect upon life after death and the history of Islam while visiting.

HISTORICAL FIGURES BURIED HERE

Hazrat Abu Talib

Abdul Muttalib
