Jabal-e-Rehmat (Mount of Mercy)

Jabal-e-Rehmat is a small hill located in the center of the Plain of Arafat. It is famously known as the place where Prophet Muhammad ﷺ stood and delivered the Farewell Sermon to over 100,000 companions. This sermon established the core principles of human rights, equality, and justice in Islam.

Historical traditions also suggest that this was the place where Hazrat Adam (AS) and Hazrat Hawa (AS) were reunited on Earth after being sent down from Paradise. Therefore, it is a symbol of forgiveness, mercy, and the reunion of humanity with their Creator.

During the day of Arafat (9th Dhul Hijjah), pilgrims gather around this hill to engage in intense prayer and supplication. While it is not a mandatory requirement to climb the hill, many pilgrims do so to reflect on the Prophet's ﷺ final message.

Historical Significance

  • The Farewell Sermon: The site where the Prophet ﷺ declared: "No Arab has superiority over a non-Arab, nor a white over a black, except by piety."
  • Prayer at Arafat: Spending the day here (Wuquf) is the most essential part of Hajj; without it, the Hajj is considered incomplete.
  • Pillar of Remembrance: A white stone pillar stands at the top of the hill to mark the location.
  • Atmosphere: On the day of Hajj, the entire hill and the surrounding plains turn white as millions of pilgrims in Ihram gather for prayer.

SPIRITUAL REFLECTIONS

Divine Forgiveness

It is believed that on the Day of Arafat, Allah forgives more people than on any other day of the year.

DAY OF MERCY

Universal Brotherhood

The sermon delivered here remains a timeless manifesto for human equality and the protection of women's rights.

The Climax of Hajj

Standing at the foot of Jabal-e-Rehmat symbolizes the gathering of all mankind on the Day of Judgment.
