Samarra Ziyarat Guide

Samarra is a historic city in Iraq and home to the shrine of Imam Ali al-Hadi (A.S) and Imam Hasan al-Askari (A.S).

It is also associated with the occultation of Imam Mahdi (A.J), making it a deeply significant place for pilgrims.

Spiritual Significance

  • Burial place of Imam Ali al-Hadi (A.S).
  • Burial place of Imam Hasan al-Askari (A.S).
  • Associated with Imam Mahdi (A.J).
  • Historic and sacred Islamic site.
