Kazmain Ziyarat Guide

Kazmain (Kazimain), located in Baghdad, Iraq, is a sacred site where two great Imams are buried: Imam Musa al-Kazim (A.S) and Imam Muhammad al-Taqi (A.S).

The shrine is known for its beautiful golden domes and attracts thousands of pilgrims every year.

Spiritual Significance

  • Burial place of Imam Musa al-Kazim (A.S).
  • Burial place of Imam Muhammad al-Taqi (A.S).
  • Located in Baghdad.
  • Important pilgrimage destination.
