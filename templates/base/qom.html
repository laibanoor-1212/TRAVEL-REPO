Bibi Masooma Shrine (Qom)

The shrine of Hazrat Fatima Masooma (A.S) in Qom is one of the most revered religious sites in Iran. She was the sister of Imam Ali al-Raza (A.S) and is highly respected for her knowledge, piety, and devotion.

The city of Qom is considered the spiritual center of Iran and a major hub of Islamic scholarship, attracting thousands of pilgrims and students every year.

Spiritual Significance

  • Resting place of Hazrat Fatima Masooma (A.S).
  • Sister of Imam Ali al-Raza (A.S).
  • Qom is a major center of Islamic education and scholarship.
  • Highly recommended ziyarat for pilgrims visiting Iran.
