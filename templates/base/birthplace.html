Birthplace of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

The birthplace of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is located in the Suq al-Layl area of Makkah, very close to the Masjid al-Haram. It is traditionally believed to be the exact spot where the Prophet ﷺ was born in the Year of the Elephant (570 CE).

The site originally belonged to the Hashim clan. After the migration to Madinah, the house changed hands several times. In the 20th century, to preserve the location while preventing unauthorized rituals, the building was converted into a library known as Maktabat Makkah al-Mukarramah.

While the structure seen today is not the original house from the 6th century, the location remains a focal point for pilgrims who wish to reflect on the humble beginnings of the final Messenger of Allah.

Historical Context

  • Location: Situated in the valley of She'eb Abi Talib, east of the Grand Mosque.
  • The Library: Established in 1951 to protect the site and serve as a center for Islamic knowledge.
  • Access: Visitors can view the building from the outside while walking between the mosque and the nearby hotels.
  • Significance: It marks the start of the life of the man who transformed world history.

SPIRITUAL REFLECTIONS
