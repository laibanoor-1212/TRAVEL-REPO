Karbala Ziyarat Guide

Karbala is one of the most sacred cities in Islam, located in Iraq. It is the land where Imam Hussain (A.S), the grandson of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), sacrificed his life for truth and justice in the historic battle of Karbala.

Millions of pilgrims visit Karbala every year, especially during Arbaeen, to pay tribute and seek spiritual connection with the Ahlulbayt (A.S).

Spiritual Significance

  • Resting place of Imam Hussain (A.S).
  • Burial site of Hazrat Abbas (A.S).
  • Center of the historic Battle of Karbala.
  • Symbol of sacrifice, patience, and justice in Islam.

Important Ziyarat Sites

  • Imam Hussain Shrine – The heart of Karbala.
  • Hazrat Abbas Shrine – Known for loyalty and bravery.
  • Bayn al-Haramayn – The area between the two shrines.
  • Khaimah Gah – Campsite of Imam Hussain’s family.
