Farewell Tawaf

A final farewell to the Sacred House before departing from Makkah.

What is Tawaf al-Wida?

Tawaf al-Wida, commonly known as the Farewell Tawaf, is performed before a pilgrim leaves Makkah after completing the pilgrimage.

The Final Farewell

This Tawaf provides pilgrims with a final opportunity to visit the Kaaba and remember Allah before beginning their journey home.

  • Prepare yourself before entering the Sacred Mosque.
  • Perform the prescribed Tawaf.
  • Make dua and remember Allah.
  • Complete your departure preparations respectfully.

A Meaningful Ending

The Farewell Tawaf is an emotional conclusion to the pilgrimage and reminds pilgrims to carry the lessons of Hajj into their lives after returning home.
