Masjid Aisha (RA)

Masjid Aisha, also known as Masjid-e-Taneem, is located approximately 7 km away from the Holy Kaaba. This mosque serves as a "Miqat" (boundary point) for those residing in Makkah who intend to perform an additional Umrah.

The mosque is named after Umm-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Aisha (RA). During the Farewell Pilgrimage, when Hazrat Aisha (RA) expressed her desire to perform Umrah, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ instructed her brother to take her to this very spot to enter the state of Ihram.

The mosque is quite spacious and equipped with excellent facilities for bathing (Ghusal) and ablution (Wudu), allowing pilgrims to comfortably change into their Ihram and offer voluntary prayers before starting their Umrah.

Visiting Guidelines

  • Ensure you perform Wudu or Ghusal before wearing the Ihram.
  • Offer two Rakat of Nafl prayer inside the mosque.
  • Make the formal intention (Niyyah) for Umrah and begin reciting the Talbiyah after the prayer.
  • Transport services (Taxis/Buses) are available 24/7 for the convenience of pilgrims.

KEY INFORMATION FOR PILGRIMS

Miqat Point

Residing pilgrims or visitors intending for a second Umrah enter Ihram from here.

LOCATION: TANEEM

Historical Event

Hazrat Aisha (RA) started her Umrah from this location upon the Prophet's ﷺ command.

Facilities

Spacious areas for wudu, showers, and nearby stalls for purchasing Ihram are available.

OPEN 24/7
