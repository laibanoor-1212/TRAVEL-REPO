Najaf Ziyarat Guide

Najaf is one of the holiest cities in Islam, located in Iraq. It is the resting place of Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib (A.S), the first Imam and cousin of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).

Pilgrims from around the world visit Najaf to seek blessings and spiritual peace at the shrine of Imam Ali (A.S).

Spiritual Significance

  • Burial place of Imam Ali (A.S).
  • One of the most sacred cities in Islam.
  • Center of Islamic scholarship and learning.
  • Nearby Wadi-us-Salaam (largest cemetery in the world).
