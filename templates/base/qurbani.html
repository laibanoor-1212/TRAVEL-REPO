IMPORTANT HAJJ RITUAL

Tawaf al-Ifada

A significant Tawaf performed as part of the completion of the Hajj rituals.

What is Tawaf al-Ifada?

Tawaf al-Ifada is an important ritual associated with Hajj. Pilgrims perform Tawaf around the Kaaba as part of the sacred pilgrimage.

Performing Tawaf

Pilgrims approach the Sacred Mosque and perform the prescribed circumambulation around the Kaaba while remembering Allah and making dua.

  • Prepare yourself before entering the Sacred Mosque.
  • Maintain cleanliness and respectful behavior.
  • Perform the required circumambulation around the Kaaba.
  • Follow official guidance and crowd-management instructions.

Spiritual Significance

Tawaf is an expression of devotion and submission to Allah. It brings the pilgrim's attention back to the sacred center of worship.
