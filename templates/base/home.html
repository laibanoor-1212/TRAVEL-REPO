How To Book On Safar-e-Haram

A secure 4-step booking journey designed for complete peace of mind.

01

Select & View Package

Different verified agents list their packages. Compare pricing, hotels, transport, and select the suitable option.

02

Submit Booking Request

Review full package details and submit your booking request. The travel agent will review and approve your request.

03

Pay to Escrow Hold

Once approved (or prior), pay securely. Your amount is locked safely in Escrow—it is NEVER sent directly to the agent.

04

Ticket Delivery & Release

Agent sends your travel tickets. Once you verify and confirm ticket receipt, funds are released to the agent.
