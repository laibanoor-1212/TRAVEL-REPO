Masjid al-Jinn

Masjid al-Jinn is one of the most historic mosques in Makkah. It is located near the Jannat al-Mu'alla cemetery. The mosque is built on the spot where a group of Jinn listened to the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) reciting the Holy Quran.

After hearing the divine words, they embraced Islam and pledged their allegiance to the Prophet (PBUH). This event is significantly mentioned in Surah Al-Jinn of the Quran.

Historical Significance

  • Site where the Jinn pledged their Bay'ah (allegiance).
  • Located at the spot where the Prophet (PBUH) drew a line for Abdullah bin Mas'ud (RA).
  • Frequently visited by pilgrims during Ziyarat in Makkah.
  • A testament to the universal message of Islam for both Humans and Jinn.
