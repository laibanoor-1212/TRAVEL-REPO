Tehran Ziyarat Guide

Tehran, the capital of Iran, is home to several important Islamic shrines and historical religious sites. While it is not as famous as Mashhad or Qom, it still holds deep spiritual and historical significance for pilgrims.

Pilgrims visiting Tehran can explore sacred shrines, historic mosques, and spiritual centers connected with Ahlulbayt (A.S) and Islamic history.

Famous Ziyarat Places in Tehran

  • Imam Khomeini Shrine – One of the largest religious complexes in Iran.
  • Shah Abdol Azim Shrine – A major pilgrimage site in Rey.
  • Imamzadeh Saleh Shrine – Located in Tajrish, very popular among visitors.
  • Historic mosques and Imamzadeh sites across the city.
