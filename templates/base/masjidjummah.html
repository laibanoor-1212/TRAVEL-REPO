Masjid al-Jummah

Masjid al-Jummah holds a prestigious place in Islamic history as the site where the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ performed the first-ever congregational Friday prayer. This event took place while he was traveling from Quba to the heart of Madinah.

The tribe of Bani Amr bin Auf had hosted the Prophet ﷺ in Quba for several days. As he departed for the city on a Friday, the time for prayer arrived while he was in the valley of Ranuna. There, he delivered the first Khutbah (sermon) and led approximately one hundred companions in prayer.

The mosque is also known by other names such as Masjid al-Wadi and Masjid Atikah. The modern structure is beautifully designed with a large central dome and several smaller ones, serving as a reminder of the institutionalization of Jummah as a weekly day of gathering for Muslims.

Visiting Guidelines

  • If possible, visit on a Friday to experience the spirit of the first congregation.
  • Offer two rakats of prayer and reflect on the first Khutbah delivered by the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Observe the unique architectural blend of traditional and modern Islamic styles.
  • Ensure you do not disturb those engaged in worship or recitation of the Quran.
