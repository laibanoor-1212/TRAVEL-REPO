Masjid al Mustarah

Masjid al-Mustarah is a historic mosque in Madinah where the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ rested after the Battle of Uhud. The name means “place of rest”, reflecting its significance. It is also known as Masjid Bani Haritha.

The mosque holds spiritual importance for visitors who reflect on the events of Uhud and the sacrifices of the companions. It remains a peaceful ziyarat location.

Visiting Guidelines

Visiting Guidelines

  • Offer Tahiyyatul Masjid upon entering
  • Recite duas and remember Allah
  • Maintain respect and silence
  • Reflect on the history of Uhud
