Arees Well (Well of the Ring)

The Arees Well is located near Masjid Quba in Madinah. It is historically famous as the site where the silver ring of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ fell and was lost forever during the caliphate of Hazrat Usman (RA).

This ring was used by the Prophet ﷺ to seal official letters and was inherited by the first three Caliphs. Despite an intensive three-day search led by Hazrat Usman (RA) and his companions, the ring was never recovered, marking a symbolic turning point in Islamic history.

The well and its surrounding gardens were a place where the Prophet ﷺ used to sit and relax. It serves as a poignant reminder of the personal life of the Prophet ﷺ and the deep emotional connection the Sahaba had with his blessed belongings.

Visiting Guidelines

  • Visit the site with a heart full of reflection on the Prophet's ﷺ life.
  • Learn about the history of the Prophetic seal and its significance in statehood.
  • Ensure you follow local regulations regarding access to the historical well area.
  • Respect the heritage sites nearby, including the neighboring gardens of Quba.
