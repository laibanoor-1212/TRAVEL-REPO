Riaz ul Jannah

Riaz-ul-Jannah is known as the "Garden of Heaven". The history of Riaz-ul-Jannah goes back to the early days of Islam when Masjid an-Nabawi served as a center of worship and learning for the growing Muslim community.

This was the place where the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ used to offer prayers, teach the companions, and guide the believers. Because of this close connection with the Prophet ﷺ, the place holds great spiritual significance for Muslims around the world.

When the Prophet ﷺ passed away, Muslims preserved this blessed place with great care. Today it remains one of the most sacred places inside Masjid an-Nabawi, visited by millions of pilgrims performing Hajj and Umrah.

Visiting Guidelines

  • Pray maximum Nafl in Riaz-ul-Jannah.
  • Recite Quran and make Dua.
  • Recite Morning and Evening Adhkar.
  • Send Salam to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his companions.
