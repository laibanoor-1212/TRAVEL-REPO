Masjid Abu Bakar Siddiq

Masjid Abu Bakar Siddiq is a historically significant mosque in Madinah, located near the south-west side of Masjid an-Nabawi. It stands on the site where the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ used to perform Eid prayers.

Following the passing of the Prophet ﷺ, Hazrat Abu Bakar Siddiq (RA), the first Caliph of Islam, continued this tradition of leading the Eid congregational prayers at this very location.

The mosque is named in honor of Hazrat Abu Bakar (RA) to commemorate his leadership and his dedication to preserving the Sunnah. Its architecture and serene atmosphere make it a vital stop for pilgrims.

Visiting Guidelines

  • Enter the mosque with respect and maintain silence.
  • Perform two rakats of Tahiyat-ul-Masjid upon entry.
  • Read about the life of the first Caliph to appreciate the history.
  • Avoid blocking pathways for other worshippers.
